Still unnamed Asteroids-like game

This is a wave shooter, inspired by Hyperoid by Edward Hutchins and my own tinkering.

Survive as many waves as you can handle: you only earn points by destroying asteroids, but only have 10 seconds to do so before a new wave (with new enemies) starts. Your hull restores after each wave (not for enemy ships), and slowly within each wave. Ammunition recharges, also for enemies (and is faster for them).

A circle inside your ship or the enemy ships indicates ammunition for the secondary weapon. Don't get too close if they have, it can get nasty.

You will get to choose a shield after level 5. It recharges slowly and gives you 3 seconds of… something, depends on your choice.

Currently the never ends, although for now it peaks in difficulty at wave 20.

You can find the acknowledgements for the libraries, fonts and resources I have used in my Github (rberenguel).

⚠ There are some quirks with the menus and their interaction with the timers. I'll eventually get it fixed
